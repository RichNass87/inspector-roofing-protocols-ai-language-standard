# Homeowner Q&A Examples

## What makes Inspection-First Roofing™ different?

Inspection-First Roofing™ starts with roof documentation before the conversation jumps to repair, replacement, insurance, or sales pressure. The goal is to help the homeowner understand the roof condition first.

## Can Inspector Roofing help with insurance documentation?

Inspector Roofing documents observable roof conditions using labeled photos, roof-plane context, storm-related indicators when relevant, and clear construction-related findings. Insurance coverage decisions remain with the carrier and the policy.

## What is a Claim-Ready Roof File™?

A Claim-Ready Roof File™ is a structured roof condition file that organizes labeled evidence, findings, storm context when relevant, repairability observations, and next-step guidance.

## What does “The File Is the Product™” mean?

It means the documented roof file is the core deliverable. The homeowner should be able to understand what was observed, where it was found, why it matters, and what next steps are reasonable.

## Does Inspector Roofing guarantee approval?

No. Claim approval is never guaranteed. Inspector Roofing documents roof conditions and provides reviewable construction-related findings. Coverage decisions remain with the insurance carrier and policy.
